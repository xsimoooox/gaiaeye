AI for Earth Observation

← Back to Blog

Earth observation has entered a new era thanks to the convergence of satellite imagery and artificial intelligence. At GAIAEYE, we are pushing the boundaries of what is possible by analyzing petabytes of data to reveal patterns invisible to the naked eye.

A Global View in Real-Time

Our computer vision algorithms process satellite feeds continuously, enabling instant detection of environmental changes, from deforestation in the Amazon to ice melting in the Arctic. This responsiveness is crucial for decision-makers who must act quickly in the face of climate crises.

Beyond the Visible Spectrum

AI doesn't just "see", it interprets. By combining multispectral data (infrared, radar, lidar), we can assess crop health, air quality, and even the stability of urban infrastructure.
