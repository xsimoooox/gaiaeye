Who We Are

GAIAEYE is revolutionizing humanity's relationship with the Earth through a unique alliance between space technology and artificial intelligence. Our platform harnesses advanced satellite data to decode the secrets of soils, ecosystems, and natural hazards, transforming randomness into predictive science.

Whether it's recreating the world's most renowned agricultural terroirs anywhere with TERRA-GENESIS, mapping and prioritizing survivors in disasters with our intelligent bracelets, or designing buildings in perfect harmony with their environment through TERRA-FOUNDATION, we don't just observe—we understand, model, and transform.

GAIAEYE marks the end of environmental uncertainty and the dawn of an era where every human intervention is informed, optimized, and sustainable.
